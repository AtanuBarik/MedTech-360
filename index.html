MedTech 360 is designed as a unified shell where Evalueserve CI and PMR outputs can be curated by domain, linked to source evidence and translated into decision-ready views.

MedTech 360 prototype • Evalueserve-inspired design language • Dashboard figures and benchmark scores are illustrative placeholders, not client research findings.

Design & Research Basis

This prototype uses the Evalueserve corporate visual language supplied in the project template: deep purple, bright magenta accents, white content surfaces, compact iconography, structured cards and dashboard-style data visualization.

Public Evalueserve capability alignment

The solution architecture also reflects Evalueserve's publicly described MedTech PMR and competitive intelligence capabilities, including primary data collection, market and competitor assessment, continuous competitor monitoring, product launches, regulatory approvals, partnerships, M&A, conference intelligence and strategic analysis.

  • Evalueserve — Primary Market Research, MedTech
  • Evalueserve — Market & Competitive Intelligence
  • Evalueserve case studies for medical-device PMR, orthopedic competitive intelligence and CI dashboards

Important: All numeric KPIs, scores, signal counts and benchmark values shown in this frontend are illustrative demo data. Production implementation should connect authenticated enterprise data sources, approved research repositories and governed analytics services.
